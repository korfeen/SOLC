What's gone

The post on the left and the beam under the picture with the name written on it: the frame you pick is the only frame now. The picture is the same size in every frame (320 px, was 390); the big frames hang over the post and the edge instead of shrinking it.

The showcase

No boxes or black borders: the three framed pictures hang right on the page, a little crooked, overlapping a touch like a crowded wall. Each frame sits centred in its square spot (the polaroid lifts its picture), the picture the same size in every frame. An empty spot is a chalk outline with a +.

Pictures on the right

Click a picture in the showcase: it comes up big on the right, in its frame. Under it its rarity and layers, arrows by its plate to flip through your pictures; "Hang it here" puts the one you're looking at in the spot you clicked (an empty spot opens the gallery to pick one). "Open gallery" shows every picture on the wall at once. "Back" goes back to your portrait. Dragging works as in the addon: drag any picture (gallery, strip, the big one) onto a showcase spot to hang it; drag a showcase picture onto another spot to swap, or anywhere else to take it down. Picking up a showcase picture switches the right side to the full gallery.
