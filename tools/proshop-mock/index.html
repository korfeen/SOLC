The one view

Left curtain: Ogre (skin, eyes, expression), Wear (outfit, headwear, accessory), Picture (background, frame, name, effect) and Favours. The mannequin wears the display set (club blazer, tiny top hat) with your cart on top. Keys: Up/Down or W/S tabs, Left/Right or A/D pieces, Enter adds, Esc goes back.

Picture pieces

The Picture tabs show YOUR ogre (the Overview portrait); the rest show the walnut mannequin. Frames are one collection for your portrait and minted pictures: the painted ones (free, Ogre Rank) plus the shop's (drawn by code until painted). The name colour goes on your plate and wherever SOLC shows your name. Effects live inside the portrait only: moonbeam, fireflies, gold dust, halo.

Favours

Looking at one throws it at the mannequin (click the picture to throw again). Bought in amounts (1, 5 or 10) and they stack in the cart. Shortcuts in the room: the wall of frames opens Frame, the favours shelf opens Favours.
