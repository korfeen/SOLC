# SOLC 0.42.0 is out

The Sleepy Ogre Leisure Club addon is ready for WoW Forever. Members of the guild only.

## What it does
- **Guildmates**: look up anyone in the club - their gear, professions, kills, deeds and pictures - and find who can
  craft the recipe you need.
- **Your page**: your own stats, kills and deeds, and how far you've climbed the Ogre Rank.
- **Bounty board**: new posters every week for the whole guild, with points for each one you finish.
- **Points**: earned for killing, exploring, gathering, levelling professions and more. Spend them in the Shop.
- **The Bedroom**: dream up an ogre picture of your own and show it off.
- **Games Room**: a few things to play with the club. More to come.

Type **/solc** to open it. Reginald will show you around.

## How to install
1. Install **WowUp** (wowup.io) if you don't have it.
2. In WowUp, pick your WoW Forever install at the top.
3. Go to **Get Addons** -> **Install from URL** and paste: `https://github.com/korfeen/SOLC`
4. Install, then restart WoW (a full restart, not just /reload).

WowUp keeps it up to date from then on.
